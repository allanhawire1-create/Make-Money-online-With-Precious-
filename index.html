Help Centre / FAQ

Does registration prove payment?

No. The administrator must independently verify the transaction using the payment provider before approving a record.

Will I definitely earn money?

No. This prototype cannot promise earnings, jobs, withdrawals, or referral rewards.

Are records stored online?

No. This version stores data in the current browser. It does not automatically send your records to an administrator.

Can I download my records?

Yes. The dashboard offers a download of your member information. The administrator can also export local records as CSV files.

Is the administrator PIN secure?

No. The PIN is for demonstration only. A real website requires server-side authentication and access controls.
